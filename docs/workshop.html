Workshop on Semi-Infinite and Double Affine Geometry

June 7–11, 2027, University of Glasgow

The workshop will address recent progress in double affine and semi-infinite geometry, broadly understood. Topics will include Kac-Moody groups, p-adic loop groups, semi-infinite flag varieties, Couloumb branches, and associated geometric representation theory.

Registration

The registration form is here. The registration deadline for participants applying for funding is April 6.
